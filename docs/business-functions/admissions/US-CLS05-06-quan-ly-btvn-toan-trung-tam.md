# US-CLS05-06: Quan ly tong hop BTVN toan trung tam (Homework Dashboard)

> Tai lieu dang cho bien tap

## Boi canh (Context)
- **Vi tri:** Menu doc lap tren Sidebar hoac Tab trong man hinh Kiem duyet diem danh (US-CLS05-04).
- **Muc dich:** Manager/CS xem tong quan tinh hinh lam BTVN cua toan bo hoc vien tai co so. Phat hien nhung HV lien tuc khong lam bai de co bien phap xu ly (goi dien nhac, tao ticket Care).

## De xuat Giao dien (Expected UI/UX)
- **Summary Dashboard:**
  - Ty le hoan thanh BTVN toan trung tam (%)
  - Top 5 HV khong lam bai nhieu nhat
  - Top 5 Lop co ty le hoan thanh thap nhat
- **Bang Grid chi tiet:**
  - Cot: Ten HV, Lop, So bai da giao, So bai da lam, Ty le (%), Bai gan nhat chua lam.
  - Filter: Loc theo Lop, theo Khoang thoi gian, theo Trang thai.
- **Action:** Click vao Ten HV -> nhay sang Student 360 View (US-CLS03-10: Tab Bai tap).

## Nguon du lieu (Data Provider)
- Tong hop tu ket qua BTVN cua tat ca cac buoi hoc trong co so
